# License notes

Each frozen image has complete attribution in its case manifest. All three
selected photographs carry Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 licenses. Image
licenses apply to the source photographs and remain distinct from the Open
Images annotation license.

Open Images annotations are recorded as Creative Commons Attribution 4.0:

`https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/`

The adapter accepts attribution licenses only. It rejects missing, unclear,
non-CC, and no-derivatives licenses, and incomplete author or landing-page
metadata.
